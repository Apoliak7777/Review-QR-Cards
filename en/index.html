Why a plate

Customers are happy to praise you. As long as it doesn't take six steps.

When you ask for a review in person, the customer nods and leaves. At home they would have to open Maps, find your business and click through to the review form. Most never do. The plate cuts it down to a single move while the experience is still fresh.

Without a plate 6 steps

  1. Open Google Maps
  2. Type in your business name
  3. Pick the right location from the list
  4. Go to the Reviews tab
  5. Find the Write a review button
  6. Finally leave a rating

With a ťukni plate 1 step

1They hold their phone to the plate and tap the notification. The review form for your business opens straight away.

Works on iPhone and Android, no app needed.

Google review policy

Reviews by Google's rules. Otherwise there's no point.

Google prohibits rewarding reviews and choosing who you ask for one, and it may remove such reviews. That's why the ťukni plate sends every customer straight to Google's official form, with no filters or extra steps. We'll also explain the rules during installation.

Google Maps user-contributed content policy

This is fine

  • Asking every customer for a review in the same way
  • Keeping the plate in a spot visible to all customers
  • Pointing to the plate when customers pay

Google prohibits this

  • Giving a discount, gift or reward for a review
  • Sending only happy customers to Google
  • Demanding a review or pressuring customers on site
  • Setting staff targets for the number of reviews
  • Asking for specific content, such as an employee's name
  • Reviewing your own business (owner, staff, family)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about NFC plates

Didn't find an answer? Call +421 9xx xxx xxx (Mon – Fri, 9:00 – 17:00) or write to ahoj@tukni.sk.

Does the customer need an app?

No. All it takes is an unlocked phone: an iPhone XS or newer, or an Android phone with NFC turned on. It usually is on if the customer pays with their phone. After tapping, a notification appears and one tap opens the review form.

Does the customer need a Google account?

Yes. Only signed-in users can write a Google review; that's how Google set it up. Most Android users are signed in. On iPhone the review form often opens in Safari, and if the customer isn't signed in there, Google asks them to sign in first. The same applies to the QR code.

What if the customer's phone has no NFC?

They scan the QR code with the camera. During installation we'll also put up a QR code sticker, for example next to the plate or on the menu. It leads to the same link as the chip.

Where will you stick the plate?

We choose the spot together during installation. Ideally where customers see it when paying, but not right next to the card terminal, so they don't confuse the two. The plate is stuck flat on a counter, glass or door; it is not a stand. We don't stick it directly on metal, where the chip might not work.

Where do you travel and when can you come?

Tell us where your business is. We'll get back to you within one working day and agree on the date and how we'll get to you. The installation itself usually takes no more than half an hour.

How do I pay?

Only after installation, once the plate works. We send you an invoice and you pay by bank transfer. We are not registered for VAT, so €50 is the final price for the plate including installation.

Can someone overwrite my chip?

No. We protect writing to the chip with a password, so no stranger can put a link to another website on it. Cheap stickers without protection, on the other hand, can be overwritten by anyone with a phone.

What if my business name or address changes?

The link points to your Google Business Profile, not to your business name. If you change the name or opening hours in the same profile, the plate keeps working. If you move or switch to a new profile, the link needs to change. There's no need to remove the plate; we reprogram it on site.

What if the plate doesn't work?

The chip and the link are covered by a 12-month warranty from installation. Just call or write. If the chip is faulty or the link was set incorrectly, we replace or reprogram the plate free of charge.

Does this comply with Google's policies?

Yes, if you use it correctly. Google allows you to ask customers for reviews as long as you don't offer rewards, don't ask only happy customers, don't pressure anyone and don't review yourself or through staff or family. The plate sends everyone the same way to Google's official form.

What if someone writes a negative review?

It can happen. The plate treats all customers the same, because Google's policies prohibit asking only satisfied customers. But you can publicly and factually reply to every review in your Google Business Profile. The reply appears right below the review, where other customers see it too. You can report a review that breaks Google's policies (for example, a fake or offensive one) to Google. And when many regular customers leave reviews, a single critical one gets lost in the overall average far more easily than when you only have a few.

How many reviews will I get thanks to the plate?

It depends on the number of customers and whether they know about the plate. We won't promise specific numbers. It works best when staff say at checkout: “If you'd like to rate us, just hold your phone here.”

How do I clean the plate and how long does it last?

Wipe the acrylic with a damp cloth. The chip has no battery, nothing on it wears out, and the chip maker states 10 years of data retention.

Can I get a plate with my own logo or custom text?

For larger quantities, yes. Tell us how many plates you need and we'll send you a design and a price.
